Photo Picker

Sort the trip photos and write the diary. Everything stays on this computer until you export.
  1. Choose the folder with all your trip photos (copy them off the phone first).
  2. Click a photo to pick it for the diary. Click again to un-pick.
  3. Add a place, title and a few lines for each day. Captions are optional (open a photo with 🔍).
  4. Hit Preview to see the real diary page, then Export to download the finished photos.
or choose individual photos · load a saved diary.json
iPhone tip: before copying, set Settings › Photos › Transfer to Mac or PC to Automatic so photos arrive as JPGs. HEIC photos still work here, they are just slower in Chrome and Edge.
Your work is saved in this browser as you go. If you come back later, choose the same folder and your picks and notes will still be there.
